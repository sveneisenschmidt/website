A trip day cannot be both a daily photo and a travel post while both words live in the same list. They are not competing subjects: one describes the form of the post, the other says which run it belongs to. The list has no way to express that, so you have to choose, and either choice is wrong.
Three of the fifteen topics cover almost the whole site, and none of the three is a subject. Remove them and 176 of 272 posts have no topic left at all.
