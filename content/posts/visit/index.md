My grandmother is finally out of hospital and in a retirement home, so the boys and I went to visit her. I took half the day off and we spent time together. It is an overall modern place, with decentralized living rooms, a lobby and a green outdoor area, suitable for less mobile elderly people.

<!--more-->

In my memory, conversations with my grandparents centered around the past, this time it was a lot more in the present, how the boys are doing, what is ahead for them and how we can support my grandmother in her new reality.

Unrelated, the picture is of the string lights in our garden, which collect dew at dawn.
