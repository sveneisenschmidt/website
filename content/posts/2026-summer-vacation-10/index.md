We stayed another night at Whitsand Bay and spent the day at the beach. The pools during low tide are a magnet for the boys, we carved our own rivers to have the pools stay filled up longer before eventually drying out. Nadine and Tilian played board games at dusk. Right before she first heard and then spotted a young dunnock. I finished _[The Last Day of Creation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Day_of_Creation)_, the ending was too abrupt and I am mad about how open ended it was and quick it was done.
