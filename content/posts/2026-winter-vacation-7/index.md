Last full day in Mayrhofen. A round of Hus, a Mario Kart World cup, three groups heading in different directions and Tilian finally spending his pocket money.

<!--more-->

The morning started with Tilian and his grandmother playing a round of Hus, a game I've never quite figured out. After that he and I played a cup in Mario Kart World. Back then I [wasn't a fan](/log/games/#mario-kart-world) but Tilian loves it, and playing it together is fun.

After breakfast we split into three groups. I took the boys to the [Erlebnisbad Mayrhofen](https://www.mayrhofen.at/de/erlebnisbad-mayrhofen), Nadine went on a 14 km hike through the Zillertal with Aika and my in-laws went for a walk and then shopping. We spent about three hours at the pool. Tilian went down every slide on his own while Kellin and I had a good time in the kids' pool.

{{< img src="DSC01122.jpg" alt="Kellin in a bathrobe after the pool" >}}
