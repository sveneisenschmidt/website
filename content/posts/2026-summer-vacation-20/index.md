Today was our day before the last day of vacation. The ferry departure was at 11:30am local time with arrival at 2pm in Calais. Knowingly we had a flex ticket, we are permitted to take any ferry four hours before or after the booked timeslot. We decided to take the ferry at 9:45, as a pet owner you need to be there 90 minutes early to be booked into the "pet queue". We arrived at 7:45am and to our surprise passed border control quickly and during check-in got assigned the ferry departing at 8:15am! Fifteen minutes after checking in to get to the right lane and enter the ferry. Next to three other cars we've been the last to go on board. With a time saving of more than three hours we decided to skip our overnight stay at [Tournai]({{< ref "2026-summer-vacation-2" >}}) and go the extra kilometers so we can do the final leg home tomorrow. We decided to stay again in Heiderust and arrived early around 3pm after a total ride of 390 km today. I felt exhausted due to getting up at 6am, the stress of the ferry procedures and French, Belgian and Dutch drivers. I did not think of saying it three weeks ago but the speed limits on the UK roads and the friendly way of driving of UK drivers made driving there a lot more pleasant, except the narrow roads. The ferry cruise itself was a no brainer but the sanitary areas were in a disastrous state. Oh, I lost Kellin for a minute on deck 8 which probably cost me a few years of my life.
