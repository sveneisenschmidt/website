Today we stayed at the campsite and, it being a rest day, we explored the beach.

<!--more-->

It was a lovely walk down to the beach where I spotted my first stonechat. Right at the foot of the cliffs was where Tilian, shortly after 100 m, spotted a guy with a metal detector. Tilian being Tilian was drawn immediately to him and observed him silently for 30 minutes, then started digging himself through a stone bed at a freshwater inflow. This lucky kid found coins himself without a detector - he told us this was the best day of vacation so far. Kellin played with a group of other kids at the same spot and built a dam. Nadine and me enjoyed the distraction to watch birds, waves and the busyness of the beach. I can't believe it's already day sixteen of our journey.
