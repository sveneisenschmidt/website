Hypo 1.10 is out, with better support for Nikon cameras and it's various MakerNote implementations, Fujifilm RAF files, and autofocus points on Canon bodies.

<!--more-->

Now that Hypo is out and other people are actually using it, or about to, I find myself far more critical about the feature set and where the app still needs to go. Which is a good thing. So expect a sequence of updates, most of them about camera support.

1.10 includes better camera and format support for Canon, Nikon and Fujifilm, as well as minor improvements to autofocus visualisation:

- Autofocus points on Nikon cameras, mirrorless and DSLR
- Full support for Fujifilm RAF files
- Autofocus point shown in the zoom navigator
- More accurate autofocus points on rotated shots and Canon bodies
