We stayed at Ladram Bay for the day before moving on to the next destination tomorrow. The kids spent most of the day at the beach, Nadine and I took turns around lunchtime as Aika had to stay off the beach, while I was with the boys, she went for a long hike with Aika towards the east. She spotted firecrest, goldcrest, coal tit, wren, blackcap and treecreeper. The _Canon R5_ has been doing a great job during our journey but the _EF 24-105mm f/4L IS USM_ lens stopped working reliably, after each picture the Canon errors out with a sync error between lens and body. It's time for a service at [_Calumet_](https://www.calumetphoto.de) in Leipzig.
