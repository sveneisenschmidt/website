The frost shot is not sharp, which is on me, but the color rendering is still noticeably better than what I get from the Canon. The Canon is always fitted with the telephoto lens, which limits the overall image quality, and I do not want to carry multiple lenses. So the practical answer for me is to bring both cameras.

I also spent a good part of today working on Hypo. Jasper gave me useful input, and the result is noticeably better. Over the last few days I have been using it for all my imports. With today's improvements I focused on performance of loading a large set of images, fast image comparison, and better metadata handling (spoiler: auto focus pinning), and I will write more about that soon. Notarization with Apple is still not complete, but I have local signing with my official Apple developer account, which makes the app installable for others.
