Nadine went _wolf tracking_ last weekend and took great shots. It took place across the Czech border and was organized by two wildlife academies: [Funkenflug](https://wildnispaedagogik-erzgebirge.de/) and [Heidefeuer](https://www.wildnisschule-heidefeuer.de/).

<!--more-->

A day in the woods learning to read animal tracks. Footprints, sleeping spots, droppings - all the signs animals leave behind. I like how much depth the Sony RX100 VII was able to capture. The shots turned out great, Nadine got talent!
