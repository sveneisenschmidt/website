Sparrows are both fidgy (difficult to photograph for me) but also plenty here, so it's an easy target for a daily photo if you are patient. It was raining most of the day yesterday so sparrows sought shelter in our garden in the large pine tree and under the swing. I wonder if they realize how goofy they look.
