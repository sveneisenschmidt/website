Nadine and I got to spend the day in Leipzig all by ourselves.

<!--more-->

We dropped the kids off at Nadine's parents, and my sister took Aika. In Leipzig we headed straight to [Alex](https://www.tripadvisor.de/Restaurant_Review-g187400-d3484418-Reviews-ALEX_Leipzig-Leipzig_Saxony.html) on the Alte Börse / Naschmarkt for an early vegan dinner and ordered the Schnitzel, basic but good. After dinner we swung by Calumet, where I picked up a used Sigma TC 1401 1.4x teleconverter (more on that in a later post). 

In the evening we caught [The Jury Experience](https://feverup.com/m/392285) in Leipzig. Nadine bought us tickets as a gift for my birthday last year. I was scpectical first yet enjoyed the format and can recommend it. I saw there seams to be a spinoff of this in other western countries. Found a UK and US version.

Back home we put on [Should I Marry a Murderer?](https://www.imdb.com/de/title/tt41082351/) but didn't finish it yet, there's a lot to digest.
