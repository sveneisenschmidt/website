The Canon RF 200-800mm f/6.3-9 is here, and what should have been a boring walk to test the lens turned into a stroke of luck: a swarm of bee-eaters was grazing the field, it must have been thirty of them, Tilian and I settled down in wait and watched the show. These are by far not the best pictures, either blurry or the chromatic aberration is visible. But again, bee-eaters!
