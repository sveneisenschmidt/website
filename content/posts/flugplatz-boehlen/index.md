This week the [34. Deutsche Meisterschaft im Ultraleichtfliegen](https://www.deutsche-meisterschaft-ul.de/) takes place at a local airport ([Flugplatz Böhlen](https://www.edoe.de/)). There has been visibly more activity in the air over the last days, so the boys and I went over after finding out there is free training before the championships.

<!--more-->

We snooped around and asked one of the staff if we could get closer to the machines. To our luck we got a personal tour around the place, got close to airplanes during taxi and looked into cockpits. There were a dozen airplanes standing around with another dozen expected tomorrow when the championships officially commence.

I took a lot more photos but was not happy with lighting and background sharpness, so these are only the ones I liked best:
