We transferred from Whitsand Bay to the campsite next to the [_Lost Gardens of Heligan_](https://www.heligan.com). But only after we had an obscenely large all-vegan breakfast at the [Cliff Top Cafe](https://clifftopcafe.co.uk): English breakfast, banana milkshakes, baked potato, avocado and sausage sandwiches. If you ever get there to see the beach, this is a non-negotiable stop on the way down the cliffs. Driving, even the short distance to the new campsite, was difficult being stuffed with all the nice food. Once we arrived and set up everything, we explored the area and stopped by the _Lobbs Farm Shop_ next to the gardens. The latter we are saving for tomorrow.
