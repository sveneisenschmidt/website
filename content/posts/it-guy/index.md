I have always been the IT guy, for the family, for neighbors, for friends and their acquaintances. I am glad that part is over. Most of the problems were printers and bloated Windows installations. The internet has written plenty of bad things about printers, and I believe every word, printers are user unfriendliness by design.

<!--more-->

Now and then I still am the IT guy for my father. He works most of the time from the road or from home, and he built himself a little office space on the second floor of the garage where he works on his vintage cars in his free time. I helped him set up a new camera in MS Teams (what a terrible piece of software that is) and took the chance to take a picture of his 1948 Dodge Coupe.
