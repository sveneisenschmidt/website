We spent the day and the night at our friends' place for our annual camping tradition, which usually means tents in the garden, but after the heatwave we now have a lot of rain and cold nights, which we honestly welcome, so this year it was air mattresses inside instead. The rest of the plan stayed the same: grilling, lots of games outside, games inside, and a campfire with marshmallows at the end. Going to have a relentless week ahead of me, so this was a welcome escape from home.
