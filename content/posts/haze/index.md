In the old open-pit mining area near Meuselwitz a [coal fire](https://www.lmbv.de/bergbaufolgen/bergschaeden-sperrungen/kohlebrand-meuselwitz/) has been smoldering underground since the end of June, complete with an official [smoke warning](https://warnung.bund.de/meldungen/alt/mow.DE-TH-G-W109-20260628-000_20260628084139/Starke_Rauchentwicklung_MeuselwitzMumsdorf) for the area. The smoke carries a good ten kilometres into our village, the air smells burnt, it scratches the throat and hazes over the floodplain.

If it is like this at ten kilometres, I do not want to imagine what it is like for the people living right next to it, or for everyone across Europe dealing with real wildfires this summer.
