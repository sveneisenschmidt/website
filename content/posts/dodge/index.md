My father restores vintage cars as a hobby, and today we did a photo shoot together. The car is a 1932 Dodge Eight Series DK, fully restored by him.

<!--more-->

{{< img src="IMG_3411.JPG" alt="1932 Dodge Eight Series DK parked under willow trees" >}}

> The Dodge Eight Series DK was built in 1932, one of 27,229 Dodge vehicles produced that year. Positioned as a luxury car in the middle of the Great Depression, it came with a straight-eight engine, floating engine mounts to reduce vibration, and a top speed of around 70 mph. [More on the 1932 Dodge Eight Series DK.](https://journal.classiccars.com/2020/09/18/pick-of-the-day-already-restored-1932-dodge-dk-eight/)
