I went back to the woods looking for the [white albino deer](/posts/daily-photo-dawn/), and found a white albino doe instead.

<!--more-->

{{< img src="IMG_1329.jpeg" alt="Great egret on the meadow" >}}

A friend of Nadine's who grew up in the area told us the story: the white albino deer and several others escaped from the nature reserve during the floods in 2012. I spotted the white doe between the trees, along with a grey heron and a great egret out on the fields. In the nature reserve I found a group of young deer and does.
