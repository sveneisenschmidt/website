Last week brought a bunch of new games and demos. Pragmata was my favorite and a day one purchase for me.

<!--more-->

{{< img src="DSC01204.jpg" alt="Pragmata on PS Portal" >}}

The [Pragmata Sketchbook Demo](https://store.playstation.com/de-de/product/UP0102-PPSA33065_00-PRAGMATADEMO0000) was great. I love the mix of exploration, combat and light puzzle mechanics. It's a blend of Dead Space, The Surge and Resident Evil. I played it through cloud streaming and Remote Play on the PS Portal, worked well.

[Starship Troopers: Ultimate Bug War!](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2321780/Starship_Troopers_Ultimate_Bug_War/) was fun, the demo has one open-world level and it's from the developers that did Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun. Runs well on Steam Deck.

[REPLACED](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4261860/REPLACED_Demo/) was fun too. Jasper describes it as a mix of Inside, Half-Life and Prince of Persia, that's pretty accurate. The game crashed on the Steam Deck before I could finish it but I can see myself picking it up at release.

[God of War: Sons of Sparta](https://www.playstation.com/de-de/games/god-of-war-sons-of-sparta/) I'm going to refund. It doesn't feel right, something I can't pinpoint makes it feel off for me. This should have been a PC handheld / Steam Deck game and gone through Early Access with more end-user testing incorporated.

[Mecha Force](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4036130/Mecha_Force/) I didn't like. It feels unfinished and incoherent.

Blizzard released a Diablo 2 expansion after 25 years: [Reign of the Warlock](https://eu.shop.battle.net/de-de/product/diablo_ii_resurrected_reign_of_the_warlock#optLogin=true), with a new class, the Warlock - a hybrid of Sorceress and Necromancer, my two most-disliked classes in ARPGs.
