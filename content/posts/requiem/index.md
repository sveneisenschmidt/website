Also worth recommending: [GEE](https://www.geemag.de/), a German games magazine focused on culture and background rather than reviews. It does not use rating scores. It ran from 2003 to 2013 and came back in 2022. It publishes two issues per year, though the schedule has not always been consistent. It is aimed at longtime gamers. I am trying to collect all issues of the comeback run, but the first issue from 2022 is already out of print and I have not been able to find a copy.
