Technically impressive on the PS5 Pro and overall a recommendation. Not a continuation of RE7 or RE8 in spirit, a light homage to RE2 and RE3 with a nod to RE8. RE8 was the stronger game for story and set pieces. The first five hours are better than the last five. Levels start long and get shorter toward the end. Finished in 10.5 hours.
