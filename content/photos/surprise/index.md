While walking Aika a woodpecker landed right next to us, and all three of us were equally surprised by how close we were. I had the Canon RF 200-800mm f/6.3-9 with me and was lucky enough to get a quick snapshot.

Also, I've been exporting my photos in the wrong color format, Display P3 instead of sRGB, so I recalculated all photos on the site with [a small script](https://github.com/sveneisenschmidt/website/blob/main/scripts/to-srgb.sh). Hugo drops the color profile when it resizes images, so the browser read them as sRGB and everything looked washed out, they should look a lot better now. I have no idea why I did not see the difference earlier, I do now.
