All the parts for the pipe run from the garden house roof into the [cistern next to the sandbox](/2026-06-09-sandbox/) are finally together, after I successfully put the job off for a long time. 
The last bit was bringing the pipe connections to the right diameter, with a bench vise, an exhaust pipe expander and the T-piece itself. Mission accomplished, after a lot of effort and brute force. It does not make any sense to me that the parts we needed like a T- or Y- pipe you can't get in the local DIY stores and the stuff from Amazon need to be brute-forced into shape.
