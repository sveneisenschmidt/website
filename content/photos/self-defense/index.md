Nadine started going to an open self-defense class in a village nearby with friends, and the same trainer also offers self-defense for kids, so Tilian and I had the chance to go there together on Saturday. It was an open training, the trainer showed us a few things from boxing, how to dodge punches, how to duck and how to defend yourself from there. Most of it was pair exercises, so Tilian and I could do a lot together, and I let him hit a bit harder now and then. All in all it was a great bonding exercise, Tilian clearly had a lot of fun and was pretty worn out afterwards, he went to bed early and slept in.

I'm also happy that Nadine found a cool activity she can do in her free time. Of course I got to be the test subject right away for all the new holds and defense moves she had learned, so let's see what the future brings for me.
