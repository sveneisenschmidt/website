After finishing Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots I was tempted to feel how a Metal Gear plays on a Game Boy, so I am playing Metal Gear: Ghost Babel on my modded Game Boy Advance, a gift from [Jasper](https://jasper.tandy.is) a few years ago, while I wait for the Retroid Pocket Nova.

<!--more-->

Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots now ranks equal to Metal Gear Solid Delta: Snake Eater and Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain as one of my favorite games. I quickly got over the clunky controls, the port to the PS5 is great, and I played most of it on the PlayStation Portal, where the recent updates made the streaming very smooth.

This weekend I am picking up Control Resonant too, the reviews were very positive. I felt I could not get over that it is melee only, but then I remembered that I had fun with both Star Wars Jedi games. Ace Combat 8 releases next week as well.
