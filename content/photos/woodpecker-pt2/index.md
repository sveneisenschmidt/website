I spotted a woodpecker couple roaming around my favorite tree outside of the village, the same place I photographed the [young kestrel](https://sven.eisenschmidt.website/posts/daily-photo-warming-up/). I sneaked up behind a bush and held still for a few minutes until the male woodpecker landed 3m in front of me. Shots like this make up for weeks of dissatisfaction with my own photos.
